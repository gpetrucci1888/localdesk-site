Who we are

Localdesk is a trading name of Gio Petrucci, a sole trader based in the UK ("we", "us"). We provide AI receptionist services and websites to local service businesses.

Contact: hello@localdeskai.co.uk

The two ways we handle personal information

  • As a controller for people who visit our website, contact us, book a demo or become a client. We decide how that information is used. This policy covers it in full.
  • As a processor for people who phone our clients' businesses. When Localdesk answers a call for a client, the client is the controller and we process the caller's details on their behalf and on their instructions. The client's own privacy notice also applies. See If you've phoned a business that uses Localdesk.

Cookies and advertising

Our website host, Cloudflare, processes basic technical information such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from attacks.

If you agree in the cookie banner, we use the Meta Pixel (from Meta, which runs Facebook and Instagram). It tells us which of our adverts brought you here and whether you clicked to book a demo, booked one, tried the live demo or used the calculator. Meta may use this to measure and improve our adverts, and in line with its own privacy policy. If you click "No thanks", the Meta Pixel doesn't load. You can change your mind any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of our homepage.

We remember your cookie choice, and which advert brought you here, in your own browser. If you came from one of our adverts, we pass the advert's campaign name to Calendly when you book, so we know which advert worked. Lawful basis: your consent.

When you book a demo, Calendly's booking window opens on our site, and Calendly's own privacy policy and cookies apply to it.

If you use the live demo on our website

Our website lets you talk to our AI receptionist in your browser. If you use it:

  • Your voice is sent to Vapi and its AI speech and language providers so the receptionist can hear you and reply. Nothing is sent until you press the button and allow your microphone.
  • The conversation may be recorded and transcribed. We use recordings and transcripts only to check and improve the demo, and we delete them within 90 days.
  • Please don't share sensitive personal information in a demo call.

Lawful basis: legitimate interests (showing how our service works and improving it).

If you've phoned a business that uses Localdesk

Some businesses use Localdesk to answer calls they can't take themselves. When that happens:

  • We collect what you tell the receptionist, such as your name, phone number, address, the work you need and how urgent it is, along with the number you called from.
  • The call may be recorded and transcribed. You are told this at the start of the call.
  • We send a summary, transcript and recording to the business you called, so they can get back to you.
  • We use your details only to provide this service to that business. We never sell them or use them to market to you.

The business you called decides how your information is used, so please contact them first about your rights. You can also contact us and we'll pass your request on and help them deal with it.

Who we share information with

We use trusted service providers to run Localdesk. They may only use your information to provide their service to us:

  • Twilio, for phone numbers, calls and text messages
  • Vapi, and the AI voice, speech and language providers it uses, to answer calls (including website demo calls) and produce transcripts
  • Calendly, for booking demos
  • Meta Platforms, for measuring our adverts (only if you accept cookies)
  • Cloudflare, which hosts our website
  • Our email provider
  • Our accountant and HMRC, for tax purposes

Some of these providers store data outside the UK, including in the USA. Where they do, we rely on the UK's adequacy regulations (including the UK–US data bridge) or the International Data Transfer Agreement and other safeguards approved under UK law.

We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep it

  • Call recordings and transcripts: 90 days, unless the client asks us to delete them sooner or keep them longer for a specific reason
  • Call summaries sent to clients: for as long as the client uses Localdesk, then deleted within 30 days of the account closing
  • Enquiries that don't lead to work: up to 2 years
  • Client contracts, invoices and payment records: 6 years after the end of the tax year they relate to

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong
  • ask us to delete your information
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it
  • ask for your information in a portable format
  • withdraw consent, where we rely on it

Email hello@localdeskai.co.uk to use any of these rights. We'll respond within one month.

Keeping it safe

We use providers with strong security, protect accounts with strong passwords and two-factor authentication, and limit access to the people who need it.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.
