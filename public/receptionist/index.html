What you might want to know.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your existing number and forward calls to Localdesk when you can't pick up.

What if it can't answer a question?

It takes a message and sends you the details, so you can call the customer back.

How does it know about my business?

We build it around you: your services, areas, opening hours and how you like calls handled. You approve it before it goes live.

Do you cover my area?

We're based in Saltash and work with trades across Cornwall, Plymouth and West Devon, from Bude and Launceston to Truro, Falmouth and Penzance. Because it all runs over the phone, we can set you up anywhere in the UK.

How long does setup take?

Usually within a few days of signing up.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial on your own phone. We set everything up first, then you sign up with your payment details on the day it goes live, and nothing is charged until day 8. Cancel before then and you pay nothing.

Am I tied into a contract?

After your 7-day free trial, there's a 3-month minimum, then it rolls month to month and you can cancel with 30 days' notice. See our terms.

What does it cost?

£397 a month with no VAT to add, including 300 minutes of calls, which is plenty for most trades. Extra minutes are 25p each, and we'll tell you when you reach 80% of your minutes. The £497 setup fee is waived for our first 5 founding clients.

Will customers know it isn't a person?

It introduces itself as the virtual receptionist and never pretends to be a person. Callers can ask for a call-back from you at any time.

Is this an answering service for tradesmen?

Yes. It's a call answering service built for trades: roofers, builders, plumbers, electricians, heating engineers and more. When you can't get to the phone, it answers as your business, takes the job details and texts them to you.
