Questions

Good to know.

How quickly can you come out?

For leaks and storm damage we aim to make things safe as soon as we can, often the same or next day. For quotes, we usually visit within a week.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Surveys and written quotes are always free, with no obligation.

Are you insured?

Yes, we carry full public liability insurance and we're happy to show you the certificate before we start.

What guarantee do I get?

New roofs and re-roofs come with a 10-year workmanship guarantee. Repairs are guaranteed for 12 months.

Do I need scaffolding?

For many small repairs we can work safely from ladders or a roof ladder. For bigger jobs we arrange scaffolding, and it's included in your quote so there are no surprises.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer or card. For larger jobs we take a small deposit for materials and the rest when you're happy with the work.
