About the monthly books-check

Each month Books flags missing receipts and anything else that needs your attention, such as expenses to tidy or overdue invoices, and texts you a summary. It points things out so you can sort them. It doesn't check that your accounts are right, certify them, or replace your accountant. Which flags you get depends on your accounting software: see what works where.

Get started

What you might want to know.

Do I need accounting software already?

You need a QuickBooks Online, FreeAgent or Xero account. If you don't have an account we'll help you start one, and set it up properly for a trade business.

Does it replace my accountant?

No. Books keeps your records tidy so your accountant has less to do. They still do your tax return, VAT return and advice.

Is my information safe?

Your accounts stay in your own accounting software. Books only keeps a small amount of its own data, and we never sell it or use it for advertising. See the Books privacy policy.

What if it files something wrong?

Tap Undo in the app, or tell us. Books also flags anything that needs a look, and your accountant still checks your books and does your tax.

How do I pay?

By card or Direct Debit through Stripe, with a secure link we send once your agreement is signed. The setup fee is paid before we start, then £150 a month in advance.

Can I stop?

Yes. Give 30 days' notice by email. We disconnect Books and delete our copy of your details within 30 days. Everything in your accounting software stays yours. See the Books terms.

What support do I get?

Phone and email in normal working hours, Monday to Friday. For something urgent, text us and we'll get back to you the same working day.

Who will I deal with?

Me, Gio Petrucci, directly: from the first chat to setup and any changes after that. No call centres, no ticket queues.
