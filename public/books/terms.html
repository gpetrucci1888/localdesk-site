What Books does

Books connects to your QuickBooks Online company to help you keep your records: filing receipts, suggesting categories, creating and chasing invoices, and sending you a monthly summary and a pack for your accountant. Some features, such as quotes and profit per job, are extras that are only switched on if your agreement includes them. It works with your own QuickBooks subscription, which you pay Intuit for separately.

What Books doesn't do

  • Books is not tax or accounting advice. Figures such as "put aside for tax" are rough guides only. Your accountant is responsible for your tax returns and advice.
  • We don't submit anything to HMRC for you.
  • We don't review your books for mistakes. Books flags what is missing or untidy, and your accountant checks the rest.

Your part

  • Books files receipts automatically and only asks you when something doesn't add up. Glance at what it files and tap Undo if it's wrong. Books also flags anything that looks missing or untidy, such as an expense with no receipt. It does not check that your books are right: that is for you and your accountant.
  • Keep your records as HMRC requires. Receipt photos saved in QuickBooks normally count, but check with your accountant.
  • Only send invoices and reminders to people you actually do business with.
  • Keep your phone locked. Once a phone is logged in to Books it stays logged in. If you lose it, tell us and we'll log it out.
  • Only share accountant-pack links with your accountant. Anyone with the link can view that period's figures.
