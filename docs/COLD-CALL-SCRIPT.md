# Cold-call script for trades businesses

A guide in your own words, not a script to read out. It uses only claims already on the website. Before every call: the number is checked against TPS (and CTPS for companies) and ticked on the lead in HQ (see `docs/COLD-CALLING-COMPLIANCE.md`). After every call: update the lead in HQ.

## Opening (15 seconds)
"Hi, is that [name]? It's Gio, I'm a project manager in construction and I've started a small company called Localdesk in Saltash. Have you got 30 seconds?"
- If they're busy or say no: "No problem, sorry to interrupt. Thanks for your time." Stop. Mark the lead (not interested, or ring back if they said so).
- If they say "don't ring me again": "Understood, I won't. Sorry for the bother." Tick **do not call** in HQ straight away.

## The reason (20 seconds)
"A lot of trades miss calls when they're on a roof or under a sink, and the customer rings the next person. We've got an AI receptionist called Mia that answers when you can't, takes the caller's name, number, address and what the job is, and texts you the details so you can ring them back. You keep your own number."

## The question
"How do you handle calls when you can't pick up at the moment?" Then listen. Their answer tells you whether it's a fit.

## If there's interest: ask for the Zoom
"It's easier to hear than describe. I can show you in a 15 to 20 minute Zoom: you'd hear Mia take a call as one of your own customers. When suits you this week?"
- Agree a day and time, and the best way to send the link (only message them if they agreed to that).
- In HQ: move to **Zoom booked**, set the Zoom date and time, and the next action.

## Common answers (honest ones)
| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I've got voicemail" | "That works for some people. Mia takes the details and texts them to you, so you know who rang and what they need before you call back." |
| "Is it a robot?" | "Yes, she's an AI and she says so on the call. She takes details and passes them on. She doesn't quote or promise times." |
| "How much?" | "£397 a month with a 7-day free trial. The setup is normally £497 and it's free for our first five founding clients. No VAT, we're not VAT registered." (Check HQ for founding places left before you say it.) |
| "Does it take payments or book jobs?" | "No. It takes the caller's details and your urgent flag, and you ring them back. I'd rather tell you that now." |
| "Will customers hate it?" | "Some prefer a person. It tells them it's an AI, takes their details, and you call back. The Zoom lets you judge it yourself." |
| "I'll think about it" | "Of course. Shall I send you the demo number so you can hear her first? It's 01752 741686." (Only if they say yes.) |
| "Can I try it?" | "Yes, there's a 7-day free trial. I set it up around your business first, and you decide after." |
| "I'm on the TPS" | Apologise and end the call. Tick the TPS result in HQ so the number is never rung again. |

## Do not say
- That it answers "every call" or "never misses" one.
- Anything about results, savings or other customers (we have none yet to quote).
- That it quotes prices, books diaries or promises times.
- That QuickBooks, FreeAgent and Xero are all live for Books unless you've checked: say QuickBooks, with FreeAgent and Xero being added.

## After the call (30 seconds, in HQ)
Stage, notes, **Next action** and date, Zoom time and link if booked, and the TPS result. If they asked not to be rung, tick **do not call**.
