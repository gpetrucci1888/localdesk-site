# Sales workflow in HQ (cold calls to Zoom demo to signed up)

HQ Leads already was a pipeline with notes, a follow-up date and a draft message. It now uses these stages, nothing else new:

**Called or contacted, Interested, Zoom booked, Demo attended, Follow-up or trial, Signed up (onboarding), Paying.** Anyone can be marked Lost.

| You do | In HQ |
|---|---|
| Find a trade business to ring | Leads, Add a lead (name, number, business, "phone call"). Open it, set the **TPS and CTPS check** in Call plan. If registered, don't ring. |
| Ring them | Press Call. Afterwards move the lead to **Called or contacted**, add notes, and set **Next action** and the follow-up date. |
| They want to see it | Move to **Interested**. If they agree to a Zoom, set the Zoom date, time and link (optional) and move to **Zoom booked**. |
| Zoom demo | Move to **Demo attended**. The lead's checklist and draft message guide the next step. |
| They want to go ahead | **Make a payment link** (founding or full price), send it yourself, then move to **Signed up (onboarding)**. Follow `docs/MIA-ONBOARDING.md`. |
| Not interested or "don't ring again" | Mark **Lost**. For "don't ring again" also tick **do not call**. |

No emails or texts are ever sent by HQ. Where messages are drafted, you copy them and send them yourself, and only to people who agreed to hear from you that way.
